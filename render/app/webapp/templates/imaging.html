Imaging & radiomics

How AVAIL turns raw imaging into tabular modalities — and open datasets you can download and inspect.

Processing pipeline

1
Preprocess (AFNI): T1 → MNI registration (robust LPC two-pass), EPI motion/band-pass cleanup (0.01–0.10 Hz), 6 mm smoothing.
2
Connectivity: Schaefer-100 (17-network) atlas → full & partial correlation (Ledoit-Wolf), Fisher-z, within/between network summaries.
3
Anatomical radiomics: PyRadiomics on the MNI T1 inside the Schaefer mask — whole-brain (wb_*) and per-network (net_*) first-order, texture and shape features (IBSI, bin width 25).
4
Functional radiomics: ALFF / fALFF / ReHo maps (float-safe, coverage-masked) → texture features (alff_*, falff_*, reho_*, bin width 0.05).
5
Assemble: one row per subject → the standard metadata.tsv + features/*.tsv layout → the same AVAIL benchmark as every other modality.

avail-omics radiomics --data-root mydata --out mydata/features
avail-omics run-generic --data-root mydata --out results

Full technical notes: IMAGING_ADAPTER.md ↗

Two ways to process rs-fMRI: (a) BOLD already registered to a template space (MNI) → the runner/cloud extracts connectivity automatically, exactly as in the downloadable tables below; (b) raw native-space fMRI → run the documented AFNI registration step once (see IMAGING_ADAPTER.md), then the same extraction. The downloadable FC table below is the output of this exact pipeline on ds004392.

Process your own imaging data

Upload a ZIP with metadata.tsv (SampleID + Group) plus, per subject, a 4D BOLD NIfTI named after the SampleID (e.g. sub-001_task-rest_bold.nii.gz) or a DICOM folder named sub-001. Set radiomics below to also extract PyRadiomics features from T1 volumes.

After validation you choose where it runs: on your own computer (local runner, no upload limits) or the free cloud. Template-space (MNI) BOLD is extracted automatically; native-space runs need the documented AFNI registration step first. Uploads through the tunnel are capped near 100 MB — large cohorts: use the local runner.
