Comparison suite

These analyses are available as optional checkboxes on your job page. They run on your own data after the main pipeline finishes - select the ones you want before starting the run.

Available comparisons

1 · Missing-data benchmark (14 strategies)

Complete-case, zero/mean/median/KNN/MICE/MissForest/SoftImpute imputation (leak-free, fold-internal), early fusion, and ablations - all against the availability-aware fusion on the same data.

2 · Bootstrap CIs + pairwise model comparison

95% confidence intervals for every meta-learner and bootstrap significance tests between the top models.

3 · Missingness-recovery benchmark

On fully-measured subjects, modality layers are artificially dropped (10-70%) and the framework is compared against complete-case analysis and reference-fitted imputation.

4 · Automatic hyperparameter tuning (optuna)

Bayesian search with nested cross-validation - tuned vs prespecified hyperparameters.

5 · Calibrated fusion

Isotonic calibration of every base learner before fusion - raw vs calibrated probabilities.

6 · Ordinal-aware fusion

Embeds the class ordering (HC < MCI < PDD) in the meta-learner loss - useful when labels are ordinal.

7 · Availability-aware prediction sets

Mondrian conformal prediction with per-availability-pattern coverage - honest uncertainty: fewer modalities, wider (but valid) prediction sets.

8 · Decision curves (net benefit)

Net-benefit curves for the extreme class pair and the middle-class one-vs-rest target across decision thresholds - shows when acting on the model beats treat-all and treat-none.
