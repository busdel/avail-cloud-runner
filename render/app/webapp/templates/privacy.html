Privacy notice

Research instance. Upload only de-identified or derived omics data. Do not upload names, identifiers, MRN/TCKN, dates of birth, contact details, or personal health information. This is not a medical device.

1. Scope

This notice covers the public AVAIL-AI research interface: a fixed, de-identified demo cohort and optional uploads of de-identified or derived omics data. Patient identifiers and personal health information must not be submitted.

2. Data we process

  • Technical request data: IP address, user-agent, timestamp, requested URL and response status, generated by the web server and the tunnel provider.
  • A session cookie used only to keep you signed in; it contains no health data and is not used for advertising or analytics.
  • Demo job metadata (job id, timestamps, status) for the fixed demo dataset.
  • Uploaded de-identified/derived omics files, processed transiently to produce results. Raw uploaded files are deleted within 24 hours; raw values are not written to application logs.

3. Purpose and legal basis

We process this minimal technical data to operate and secure the service, prevent abuse, run the analysis requested by the uploader, and demonstrate the research software. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in operating a secure research service and, for uploads, the uploader’s request and attestation that the data are de-identified/derived. Essential cookies are strictly necessary for the service.

4. Retention

Server and tunnel logs are retained for up to 30 days for security and troubleshooting. Uploaded jobs and their outputs are deleted within 24 hours unless a shorter retention applies; demo outputs are temporary and may be removed on restart.

5. Recipients and transfers

The service is hosted on cloud infrastructure and exposed through a secure tunnel provider. Those providers may process technical data in other countries under their standard data-protection terms. We do not sell data and we do not use third-party analytics or advertising trackers.
